The Problem

Web3 Breaks for
Regular Users

Every blockchain action on Ethereum requires ETH for gas. Most people don't have it, don't know how to get it, and give up before taking any action.

⛽

Gas Wall

Users need ETH in their wallet before doing anything on-chain. No ETH = blocked, no exceptions.

😕

Complex Onboarding

Buy ETH on exchange → KYC → bridge → configure gas limit → approve. Most give up at step one.

🚫

Broken UX

Apps lose 90% of users at the "pay gas" step. Real utility never reaches real people.

The Solution

One Chat.
Zero ETH Required.

AgentX uses AI to understand your intent, then UGF executes the transaction — paying gas with Mock USD, never touching your ETH.

01

Connect Your Wallet

Connect MetaMask or any WalletConnect wallet on Base Sepolia. Sign a message to authenticate — no ETH needed at all.

02

Type What You Want

Type naturally: "Mint a badge for Jay" or "Donate 5 USD". The AI parser understands your intent instantly.

03

UGF Handles the Gas

UGF quotes the cost in Mock USD, settles the payment, and executes the transaction on-chain. You pay cents. No ETH.

04

Action Confirmed On-Chain

Your NFT is minted, badge is claimed, or donation is sent on Base Sepolia. Real on-chain action, with a verifiable tx hash.
